### What the disagreements may be caused by

- Thin top-flight history: 2 of the top-5 disagreements involve a team with <100 league matches in the DB (no second-division data) or a prior-only team; the market additionally prices form, injuries and lineups the model cannot see (phase-5/6 job).
- Low-scoring matchups disagree through the teams' low RATES, not through rho: the Dixon-Coles tau only moves mass inside the total-goals bands 0, 1 and 2 while preserving the marginals, so P(total >= 3) - and with it over 2.5 - is exactly rho-invariant (tested in tests/test_dixon_coles.py).
- The 50/50 goals/xG mix smooths hot/cold scoring streaks more than the market does.
- Some consensus de-vig values rest on few books (see the source column); Pinnacle is available for a minority of events.
